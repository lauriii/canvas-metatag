## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: What a product varies by is chosen as checkbox rows

A product's Variants sheet MUST offer Colour, Size, Material and Pattern as
checkbox rows, each showing its field under them once ticked.

#### Scenario: Varies by

- **WHEN** a builder opens the Variants sheet
- **THEN** Colour, Size, Material and Pattern MUST be checkbox rows, and ticking Colour MUST show a Colour field under them
